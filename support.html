Using Kept

Make a Promise

On Today, make a Promise and enter the minimum action that will count. You can make a one-off Promise or a repeating Promise on the weekdays you choose. You can keep more than one Promise on the same day, and you can plan a one-off Promise or the start of a repeating Promise up to seven days ahead. Target and Extra are optional and are not requirements.

Resolve a Promise

Choose Kept or Missed for each Promise. Nothing is marked Missed automatically. You can return to earlier unresolved Promises without blocking today.

Repeating Promises

You can pause a repeating Promise and choose when it returns. You can make its minimum smaller, change its weekdays, or archive it without rewriting the past.

Reminders and check-in

You can add one optional local reminder to a one-off or repeating Promise. Notification text stays generic and never shows your Promise. A separate evening check-in is available for days that remain unresolved. You can also return through a Home Screen widget or an App Shortcut.

Widgets and Settings

Lock Screen widgets are private and count-only, and they never show your Promise text. From the Settings tab you can choose System, Light, or Dark appearance.

Review your Evidence

Evidence shows your chronological history and overall Reliability, including each repeating Promise and each Monday–Sunday week. You can open a monthly Evidence calendar and any past day. You can reuse a past or archived Promise as an editable new draft without changing history. You can run one bounded 14-day experiment when you want to test a deliberate change.

After a Missed Promise

You can add optional factual context after a Missed Promise.

Languages

Kept follows the language selected for the app in iOS: English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.

Frequently asked questions

Does Kept require an account or internet connection?

No. Kept has no account and its core experience works offline.

Where is my data stored?

Your Promise data is stored in Kept’s private app container on your iPhone. Kept does not send it to us. Apple may include local app data in a device backup, depending on your iOS settings.

Can I make more than one Promise per day?

Yes. You can keep more than one Promise on the same day.

Can I return to an earlier Promise that is still unresolved?

Yes. You can return to earlier unresolved Promises without blocking today.

Can I change a Kept or Missed result?

Reusing a past or archived Promise creates an editable new draft and does not change history.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting Kept removes its local app container from that device. A copy may remain in an existing device backup until you manage that backup through your Apple settings.

Troubleshooting

  • Confirm your device is running iOS 17 or later.
  • Close and reopen Kept if a screen does not refresh as expected.
  • If the problem persists, include your iPhone model, iOS version, Kept version, and the steps that caused the problem.
  • Do not include personal Promise text unless you intentionally choose to share it. Screenshots may reveal Promise content.
