Using Kept

Make a Promise

You can make a one-off Promise, a repeating Promise on the weekdays you choose, or a flexible weekly Promise. Small examples are there to help you begin. You can keep more than one Promise on the same day, and you can plan a one-off Promise or the start of a rhythm up to seven days ahead. Target and Extra are optional and are not requirements.

Resolve a Promise

Choose Kept or Missed for each Promise. Nothing is marked Missed automatically. Closing a flexible week records its remaining days as Missed. You can return to earlier unresolved Promises without blocking today.

Repeating Promises

You can pause a repeating Promise and choose when it returns. You can make its minimum smaller, change its weekdays, or archive it without rewriting the past.

Reminders and check-in

You can add one optional local reminder to a one-off or repeating Promise. Notification text stays generic and never shows your Promise. A separate evening check-in is available for days that remain unresolved.

App Shortcuts

You can use App Shortcuts to open Today, start a Promise, or ask Siri how many Promises are open. Siri reads counts only, never your Promise text.

Widgets

Kept’s Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show counts only. They never display your Promise text. To update them, Kept shares only those counts and the dates they apply to with its widgets through a container on your iPhone.

Backup, app lock, and appearance

From Settings you can create a backup, restore it with a preview and confirmation, or export a readable history. An optional app lock uses your iPhone’s authentication. You can choose System, Light, or Dark appearance in Settings.

Review your Evidence

Evidence shows your chronological history and overall Reliability, including each repeating Promise and each Monday–Sunday week. You can search your evidence, review a month, and open a monthly Evidence calendar to revisit any past day with evidence. You can reuse a past or archived Promise, or prepare another attempt, without changing history. You can run one bounded 14-day experiment when you want to test a deliberate change.

After a Missed Promise

You can add optional factual context after a Missed Promise.

Reflections

You can add an optional reflection.

A quiet session

You can start a quiet session.

Pebble jar

The pebble jar has a shelf for past months. Motion respects Reduce Motion. The optional pebble sound is off by default.

Languages

Kept follows the language selected for the app in iOS: English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.

Frequently asked questions

Does Kept require an account or internet connection?

No. Kept has no account and no cloud service. Its core experience works offline.

Where is my data stored?

Your Promise data stays on your iPhone. Kept does not send it to us. Apple may include local app data in a device backup, depending on your iOS settings. A backup or readable history export you create is a file you keep. Kept has no cloud service that receives that file.

Can I make more than one Promise per day?

Yes. You can keep more than one Promise on the same day.

Can I return to an earlier Promise that is still unresolved?

Yes. You can return to earlier unresolved Promises without blocking today.

Can I change a Kept or Missed result?

No. Once you record Kept or Missed, that result stays in your history. You can still add optional context to a Missed result, or a reflection, and reusing a past or archived Promise or preparing another attempt does not change history.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting Kept removes its local app container from that device. A copy may remain in an existing device backup until you manage that backup through your Apple settings. A backup file or history export you already saved can remain where you saved it.

Troubleshooting

  • Confirm your device is running iOS 17 or later.
  • Close and reopen Kept if a screen does not refresh as expected.
  • If the problem persists, include your iPhone model, iOS version, Kept version, and the steps that caused the problem.
  • Do not include personal Promise text unless you intentionally choose to share it. Screenshots may reveal Promise content.
