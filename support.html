Kept

Support

Kept is a calm, local-only iPhone app for making one small daily Promise and recording honest evidence of whether you kept it.

Need help?

For support, bug reports, or feedback, email keptapp.support@gmail.com.

Using Kept

Make today’s Promise

On Today, tap Make a promise. Enter the minimum action that will count. Target and Extra are optional; only the Promise determines whether the day is Kept or Missed.

Resolve a Promise

Return to Today and choose Kept or Missed. Kept never marks a Promise Missed automatically. An older pending Promise must be resolved before making today’s Promise.

Review your Evidence

Evidence shows your chronological history, total Kept and Missed counts, and Reliability. Reliability is calculated from resolved Promises only: Kept ÷ (Kept + Missed).

Frequently asked questions

Does Kept require an account or internet connection?

No. Kept has no account and its core experience works offline.

Where is my data stored?

Your Promise data is stored in Kept’s private app container on your iPhone. Kept does not send it to us. Apple may include local app data in a device backup, depending on your iOS settings.

Can I make more than one Promise per day?

No. Version 1.0 deliberately supports one Promise per local calendar day.

Can I change a Kept or Missed result?

No. A resolved result is part of the evidence record and is final in version 1.0.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting Kept removes its local app container from that device. A copy may remain in an existing device backup until you manage that backup through your Apple settings.

Troubleshooting

  • Confirm your device is running iOS 17 or later.
  • Close and reopen Kept if a screen does not refresh as expected.
  • If the problem persists, include your iPhone model, iOS version, Kept version, and the steps that caused the problem.
  • Do not include personal Promise text unless you intentionally choose to share it. Screenshots may reveal Promise content.
