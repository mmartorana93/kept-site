Kept

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026.

Kept is a local-only iPhone app provided by Manuel Martorana. This policy explains how Kept handles information in version 23.0.

Information stored by Kept

Kept stores the following so the app can provide its core functionality:

  • your Promise text;
  • optional Target and Extra text;
  • whether a Promise is unresolved, Kept, or Missed;
  • the dates and times needed to show the correct day and history;
  • the time zone used for each day, so your history stays on the correct date when you travel;
  • the repeat schedule for a repeating or flexible weekly Promise, including the weekdays you choose;
  • when a paused repeating Promise should return;
  • an optional local reminder time and evening check-in time;
  • optional factual context you add after a Missed Promise;
  • optional reflections you choose to add;
  • whether a Promise is archived;
  • weekly and monthly reviews you complete, including an optional short monthly note;
  • a 14-day experiment you start, including a copy of the Promise text and your midpoint and closing choices;
  • notes you saved in Notes for Kept in an earlier version, which stay on your iPhone and in your backups; and
  • your app settings, such as app lock, appearance, the pebble sound, and the state of a quiet session.

This information stays on your iPhone. Archived Promises stay on your iPhone. Kept does not request or use your location. Reusing a past or archived Promise, or preparing another attempt, does not change history. Local reminders do not include your Promise text.

Information we collect

Kept does not automatically transmit your Promise data or app-usage data to us.

Apple’s App Store privacy label for Kept is “Data Not Collected.” The developer does not collect any data from this app.

Kept version 23.0 has:

  • no account or sign-in;
  • no developer-operated backend;
  • no analytics, advertising, attribution, or tracking SDK;
  • no cloud-sync feature;
  • no subscription; and
  • no AI feature.

The core experience works offline. Optional local reminders use iOS notification permission. A separate evening check-in is available for days that remain unresolved. Notification text stays generic and does not include your Promise text. If you turn on experiment reminders, Kept also sends generic midpoint and closing notifications for a 14-day experiment. They do not include your Promise text.

Backup, restore, and export

From Settings you can create a backup, restore a backup after a preview and confirmation, or export a readable history. These are files you create. They contain Promise information from your iPhone. You choose where to keep them. Kept has no account and no cloud service that receives these files.

A backup is a JSON file that can restore Kept. The readable history is a CSV file and cannot be restored. Both files can include your Promise text and private notes and are not encrypted. Restoring a backup replaces the data on this iPhone after you confirm. You can also use the share sheet to share an experiment summary or a saved note; nothing is shared until you choose a destination.

App lock

You can turn on an optional app lock. It uses Face ID, Touch ID, or your iPhone passcode through iOS. Kept never receives your Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode data; iOS only tells Kept whether authentication succeeded. App lock does not encrypt local data or device backups, and widgets, Siri counts, and files you exported remain outside app lock. You can leave it off.

Widgets

Kept’s Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show counts only. They never display your Promise text. To update them, Kept shares only those counts and the dates they apply to with its widgets through a container on your iPhone.

Siri and Shortcuts

Kept offers App Shortcuts. Asking Siri for your open Promises returns counts only, without reading Promise text. Kept handles these shortcuts on your iPhone; Apple’s Siri terms apply to voice requests.

Pebble jar

While the pebble jar is on screen, Kept uses your iPhone’s motion sensor to tilt the pebbles. Motion data is not stored. With Reduce Motion on, the jar stays still.

Pebble sound

The optional pebble sound is off by default. If you turn it on, that choice stays on your iPhone.

When you contact support

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. This happens outside the Kept app. We use that information only to respond, troubleshoot the request, protect the service, or meet legal obligations.

Support email is handled by Google. Google may process and store the message as described in the Google Privacy Policy. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address below, subject to information we must retain for legal or security reasons.

Device backups

Depending on your iPhone and Apple backup settings, iOS may include Kept’s local app data in an iCloud or computer backup. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by Kept, and we cannot access them. Apple’s own terms and privacy policy apply to Apple backup services.

Retention and deletion

Kept keeps its local data while the app and its app container remain on your device. Deleting Kept removes its local app container from that device. A copy may remain in an existing device backup until you delete or replace that backup through your Apple device or account settings. A backup file or readable history export you already saved can remain where you saved it until you delete that file.

Because Kept does not automatically transmit app data to us, there is no Kept account to close and no Kept app-data server to erase.

Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell, rent, or use your app data or support correspondence for advertising or profiling. Support correspondence may be processed by the email provider described above or disclosed when legally required; it is not shared for marketing.

This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or contact forms to this site. GitHub may process technical information needed to serve the pages under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how information is handled, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version is released. The effective date above will also be updated.
